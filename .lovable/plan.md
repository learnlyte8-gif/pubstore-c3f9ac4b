# Promote & Earn

Turn any verified PUBSTORE user into a seller for other people's products: they share a product link, someone buys, they earn a commission that lands in their existing PUBSTORE wallet.

This first build covers the full working money loop plus WhatsApp/social sharing. Campaigns, leaderboards, personal storefronts and automated fraud scoring come later.

## What sellers get

- A "Promote & Earn" switch on each product, with commission as a percentage or a fixed amount per sale.
- A live preview of the economics before switching it on: price, PUBSTORE fee from their plan, promoter commission, what they keep.
- A "Promoted sales" view in their store console: sales generated by promoters, commission paid, top promoters.

## What promoters get

A new "Promote & Earn" section (in the main menu, and in the desktop top bar) with:

- **Products** — a browse feed of promotable products: Top earning, Best converting, Trending, New, Hot deals. Each card shows the price and "Earn $X per sale".
- **Promote screen** — price, commission rate, earnings per sale, a short link, and one-tap sharing to WhatsApp (first and most prominent), Facebook, X, Instagram/TikTok copy, plus Copy link and Download image. Share text and images are generated from verified product data, using the existing ad-image generator, so prices can't be faked.
- **My promotions** — every link they created with clicks, orders and earnings side by side.
- **Sales & Earnings** — total, pending, available; each row shows the order, the commission and its state, including reversals after refunds.
- **Withdrawals** — moves available earnings into their PUBSTORE wallet, from which the existing withdrawal flow pays out. Minimum $10.

Product pages and product cards get a "Promote & Earn — earn $X per sale" action next to Buy now for eligible products.

## Money rules

- Commission is calculated on the product price only, never on delivery, and only on completed orders.
- States: pending on payment, confirmed on delivery, available after the return window (seller chooses, default 7 days), paid on withdrawal, cancelled or reversed on refunds. Nothing is deleted; reversals are recorded as their own entries.
- Buying through your own link earns nothing.
- Attribution: last promoter link clicked wins, valid for 7 days; a click also sticks to the buyer's account once they sign in, so it survives sign-up, cart abandonment and returning later.

## Admin

New "Promote & Earn" area in the admin console: promoters, clicks, attributed orders, commission generated/pending/available/paid/reversed, promoter-generated share of GMV, plus a review queue of suspicious promoters (self-purchase attempts, extreme conversion rates, high refund rates) and the ability to freeze a promoter, pause, adjust or reverse a commission. Every manual action is logged.

## Technical notes

- New tables: `promoter_profiles` (code, level, risk score, frozen flag), `promotions` + `promotion_links` (short code, campaign label, channel), `promotion_clicks`, `attributions` (visitor + user scoped, expiring), `commissions` (order-item level, states, timestamps, unique per order_item+promoter for idempotency), `commission_adjustments`, `promoter_payouts`, `promoter_fraud_events`. Products gain `promote_enabled`, `commission_type`, `commission_value`, `commission_release_days`, `min_promoter_level`. All with GRANTs, RLS (promoters read their own rows, sellers read commissions on their products, admins read all) and updated_at triggers.
- Ledger is authoritative: balances are derived views over `commissions` + `commission_adjustments`; the wallet is only credited at withdrawal time via `apply_wallet_transaction` with an idempotent reference.
- Commission engine as security-definer functions driven by order events, hooked into the existing escrow flow: pending at `pay-order`/`pay-group-buy-order` success, confirmed inside `_settle_order_escrow`, available via a `pg_cron` job once the release window passes, reversed by the refund/cancel paths.
- Currency stored per row (`currency`, `amount`, `rate`) so non-USD markets can be added without a rewrite; USD only at launch.
- `/x/:code` route + `track-promotion` edge function: records the click, sets the attribution cookie/session, then redirects to the product. Mobile deep links reuse the same endpoint.
- Sharing images reuse `src/lib/adCreative.ts` templates with the promoter's link; WhatsApp uses the existing WasenderAPI plumbing for notifications only, sharing itself is a normal WhatsApp share intent.
- Notifications (in-app + WhatsApp) on sale made, commission confirmed, commission available, payout processed, and to sellers when their product trends with promoters.
- New pages under `src/pages/promote/`, hooks in `src/hooks/usePromote*.ts`, admin panels alongside the existing ones, plan-style gating reusing `useVerification` for the level rules.
- Terms and privacy pages get promoter terms: accurate claims, no impersonation, no spam, no click manipulation.

## Rollout

Enabled for verified accounts, and initially offered on Electronics, Fashion, Beauty, Home and Accessories, so we can watch conversion and refund rates before opening it to the whole catalogue.
